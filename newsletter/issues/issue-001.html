Build that ships, infra that scales, robots that spot you.

Welcome to the first issue. This newsletter sits at the intersection of three things moving fast at once — applied AI, the infrastructure that runs it, and robotic fitness, where automation meets the human body. Short, practical, no filler. Here's what's worth your attention.

01 · Everything AI

From demos to dependable systems

The teams winning right now aren't the ones with the flashiest demo — they're the ones who wrapped a model in guardrails, evals, and a fallback path. Treat your prompts like code: version them, test them, and measure regressions before they reach users.

Try this week: add a tiny eval set (20–50 real examples) to your most important AI feature. You can't improve what you can't measure.

02 · Infrastructure

Boring infrastructure is a feature

Postgres, a queue, and a managed platform will carry you further than most realize. This very newsletter runs on a single Supabase project — a table for subscribers, row-level security locking it down, and edge functions doing the sending. No servers to babysit.

Principle: push state to managed services, keep your own code stateless, and your 3am pages drop to near zero.

03 · Robotic Fitness

Your next training partner is a model

Computer-vision rep counting, form correction from a phone camera, and adaptive programming that adjusts tomorrow's session based on today's effort — the gym is quietly becoming a robotics problem. The winning products keep a human in the loop and let the machine handle the reps, the tracking, and the nudges.

Watch: pose-estimation models now run on-device, which means real-time coaching without sending video to the cloud. Privacy and latency, solved together.
