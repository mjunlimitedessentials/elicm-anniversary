part of the ad. Missing fields get a stated assumption, never a blank.

- After state: what changes once they buy
- Objections they'll raise in the comments:

## 4. Campaign goal
- Objective: leads / sales / event registrations / app installs / awareness
- Traffic temperature: cold / warm (engaged, site visitors) / hot (cart abandoners, customers)
- Budget per day and test length:
- Formats wanted: single image / carousel / video / all
